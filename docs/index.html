dingding

A personal assistant for Gmail, Google Calendar, and Notion.

What it does

dingding summarizes inbox items, proposes replies as Gmail drafts, suggests calendar events, and helps organize notes. It runs on the owner's computer and communicates through a Telegram bot. It does not send email; the owner sends any draft from Gmail. Calendar additions and edits happen only when the owner asks.

How it handles private data

Google credentials stay on the owner's computer. A local privacy broker removes security codes and filters or redacts sensitive content before the cloud assistant receives it. Some filtered text and summaries are processed by the cloud assistant to answer the owner's requests. The filter reduces exposure but cannot guarantee it catches every private detail.
